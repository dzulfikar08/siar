Connecting accounts

Connect accounts in the dashboard, or ask your agent to call connect_account: OAuth platforms return a link to open, credential platforms (Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Nostr, WordPress and others) take their fields directly.

Until siar's shared apps are approved by Meta, Google and LinkedIn, OAuth platforms need your own developer app: save its client id and secret under Your own platform apps and register the redirect URI shown there. The setup guides cover each console step by step; they are written for self-hosting, so use the redirect URI from your dashboard instead of the one they build from SIAR_PUBLIC_URL.

  • Meta: Instagram, Threads, Facebook Pages
  • Google: YouTube, Business Profile
  • LinkedIn
  • Other platforms

Limits

Your plan sets how many accounts you can connect, how many posts per month you can schedule or publish, and how much media you can stage. Over a limit, tools return an error that says which limit and how to raise it. See Pricing.
