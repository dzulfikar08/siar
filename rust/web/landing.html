What you can hand off

You write one line. Your agent picks the tools; siar checks the platform rules and does the posting.

  • Post the launch thread to X and Bluesky tomorrow at 9.validate_post checks length, media and options for each network before schedule_post saves it, so the post doesn't fail at 9.
  • Answer the questions under yesterday's reel. Show me first.list_comments pulls the new ones; reply_to_comment sends the replies you approve.
  • When someone comments ‘menu’, DM them the link.create_automation sets up a comment-to-DM on Instagram, with human-like delays and once per person.
  • Which posts worked last month, and when should we post?post_performance compares each post with the account's usual reach; best_times finds the hours your own posts do best.
  • The 8 a.m. post failed. Why?get_post shows the platform's error for each target; retry_post sends it again.
  • Save these five ideas for next week.create_idea keeps them on the ideas board until you turn them into posts.

One set of tools for every network you post to

The same calls schedule a reel, a LinkedIn post or a Bluesky thread. list_platforms tells your agent each network's limits and options.

How connecting works today

Token-based networks (Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord, Slack and the rest) connect in a minute: paste the token in the dashboard, or let your agent do it.

Networks with a sign-in screen (Instagram, Threads, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, X) connect through your own developer app for now. The dashboard shows the exact redirect URI to register, and the guides walk through each console:

  • Meta: Instagram, Threads, Facebook
  • Google: YouTube, Business Profile
  • LinkedIn
  • Everything else

One-click connect through siar's own apps is coming, platform by platform, as Meta, Google and LinkedIn approve them.

Read the code. Run it yourself if you like.

siar is AGPL-3.0 and written in Rust: one static binary that idles at about 11 MB of memory in our CI runs. Self-hosting is free and gives you the same tools. The hosted service is for when you'd rather not keep a server up for the scheduler.
